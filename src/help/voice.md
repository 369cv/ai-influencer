---

1. Add your ElevenLabs key in **Settings → Connections → ElevenLabs**.
2. On her profile, under **Voice**, press **Pick a voice** and choose one from your ElevenLabs library.
3. Adjust stability, similarity, style and speed, and press **Preview** to hear a line. Previews use a few of your ElevenLabs characters.

The saved voice shows on her Looks card and is used for her talking content.
