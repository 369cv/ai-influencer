Failed posts are in **Content → Queue → Failed** and in **Needs you**. Each shows the posting service's reason.

Common reasons:
- **Media stored only in this browser:** posting needs public https links. Generated media from Higgsfield works.
- **Instagram or TikTok need media**, and a Reel needs a video.
- **Webhook answered 404 or 410:** the automation is off or its URL changed. Update it in Connections.

Press **Retry**. Only the services that failed are sent again.
