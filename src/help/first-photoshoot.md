1. On **Create**, choose **Photoshoot** under the box.
2. Pick the influencer from the chip in the box and, if you like, the format (9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9).
3. Describe the shoot: where she is, what she wears, the mood. Press enter.
4. Check the **plan**: the scene, each shot, the look and the format. Change any line by clicking it.
5. Press **Make it**. The button shows the credits it will use.

Results appear shot by shot. Tap the ones you like, then **Post**, **Save as look** or **Animate in Studio**. Every photo is also saved in **Content → Media**.
