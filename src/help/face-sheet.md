The **face sheet** shows her face from several angles. Muse sends her main photo and close-ups with every generation, so look number fifteen still looks like her.

- Make or remake the sheet in the **Character sheet** section of her profile.
- **Close-ups** of her eyes, lips and skin add detail for consistency.

If a result drifts, check the main photo is clear and front-facing, then remake the close-ups.
