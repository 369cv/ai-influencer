In the composer, when a post goes to more than one network, tabs appear above the caption:
- **All platforms:** the shared caption.
- **One tab per network:** typing there makes a caption just for that network. **Reset to the shared caption** removes it.

The counter shows that network's limit, for example 280 for X. A checks list under the post marks each platform as ready or explains the problem. **Add to queue** stays off until every platform is ready.

**Instagram as** Post, Reel or Story. **First comment** is posted right after the post (Ayrshare); **Move hashtags here** moves trailing hashtags into it.
