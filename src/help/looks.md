A **look** is a saved photo of the influencer in a particular outfit, setting or pose. Looks live at the top of her profile.

**Add looks** in three ways:
- **Describe it:** write the look; it opens as a photoshoot plan for her. Save the photos you like as looks.
- **From Media:** pick photos of her you've already made.
- **Upload:** photos from your device.

In a Create plan, choose a look and it's sent as the outfit and setting reference, after her face. Rename a look by clicking its name; remove it with ✕.
