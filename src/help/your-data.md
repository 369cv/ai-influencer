Muse stores influencers, posts, plans and settings **in this browser**, not on a Muse server. Keys you add are sent only to the service they belong to, through Muse's own proxy.

What that means:
- Another browser or device won't see your work.
- Clearing site data for Muse removes it.
- Generated photos and videos are hosted by Higgsfield; their links can expire. Download anything you want to keep.

Use **Export persona** on an influencer's profile (the ⋯ menu) to save her details as a file.
