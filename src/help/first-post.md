title: "Add an account and post"
collection: "start"
summary: "Link where she posts, set her schedule and send your first post."
cta: "Open Social accounts"
to: "/settings?section=accounts"
---

1. Connect a posting service in **Settings → Connections**: Ayrshare, or a webhook for Zapier, Make, n8n or your own endpoint.
2. In **Settings → Social accounts**, add her accounts (for example Instagram @handle) and pick which posting service each one uses.
3. Optional: set her **posting schedule** so new posts go into her next free slot.
4. In **Content**, press **New post**, pick media, write the caption and **Add to queue** or **Post now**.

Muse keeps the queue; the posting service does the actual posting at the scheduled time.
