When a plan finishes, each shot shows its photo.

- **Redo** remakes only that shot.
- **Tap a photo** to select it. Select several to post them together.
- **Post** opens the post composer with the selected photos and the influencer.
- **Save as look** adds the selected photos to her looks.
- **Animate in Studio** opens Studio's video mode with the selected photo as the start frame.

Every photo is also in **Content → Media**. Your recent requests are listed under the Create box, so you can reopen any of them.
