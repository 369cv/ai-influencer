While a plan is open you can:
- **Edit the title, scene or any shot** by clicking it and typing.
- **Add a shot** (up to 8) or **Remove** one.
- **Pick a look** saved on the influencer. It's used as the outfit and setting reference.
- **Change the format.**
- **Ask for a change** in the box under the plan, for example *make the last shot by the window*. This needs Claude connected; only what you asked for changes.

**Edit in Studio** opens the full Studio with the scene and shots filled in, for fine control.

**Discard** removes the plan. Nothing was generated, so nothing is spent.
