Muse has three places, plus Settings.

## Create
One box for everything you make. Pick the influencer, describe what you want and press enter. You get a **plan** first: free to change, with the cost on the button. Nothing is generated until you press **Make it**.

## Influencers
Each influencer is an identity: her face, her **looks** (outfits, settings, poses), her voice and her posting schedule. Everything you create reuses them.

## Content
Your queue, calendar, sent posts with stats, and every photo and video you've made (**Media**).

## Settings
Connections (Higgsfield, Claude, ElevenLabs, posting services), social accounts, appearance and **System check**.

**Tip:** the **Needs you** list on Create shows anything waiting on you: failed posts, runs to review, deals due and empty posting slots.
