Every request on Create becomes a **plan** before anything is made. Changing the plan is free.

The button shows an **estimate** of Higgsfield credits, for example *Make it · ~8 credits*. It is worked out from the model, resolution and number of shots. Higgsfield bills the real amount on your account, and its prices can change.

Things that change the cost:
- **Number of shots:** each shot is one image.
- **Model and resolution:** shown next to the format in the plan.
- **Redo:** remaking one shot costs one image, not the whole plan.

Claude (for drafting the shot list) uses your own Anthropic key and a small number of tokens.
