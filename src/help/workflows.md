A workflow chains steps you repeat, like *Photo to video reel*: photo, then video, then caption, then post.

1. Open a template in **Workflows**.
2. Fill in the setup: influencer, scene, start photo, deal or reference.
3. Press **Run**. The button shows the credit estimate.
4. Watch each step. **Stop** cancels a run.
5. At the **Post** step, review and edit the caption, then **Add to queue**. Nothing posts on its own.

Templates with a daily, deal or inspiration trigger run by hand for now. **Photo to talking clip** is planning-only until the voice and lip-sync steps are built.
