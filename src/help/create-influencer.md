Choose **New influencer** on Create (or go to **Influencers → +**). The wizard asks for:
1. **Name**
2. **Niche:** what she posts about
3. **References:** optional photos that guide her look
4. **Story:** who she is, her vibe
5. **Look:** face, hair, body, style, with a live preview
6. **Generate:** her main photo

Afterwards, her profile is where you add looks, a face sheet, close-ups and her voice.
