Muse generates photos and videos through **your own Higgsfield account**. You pay Higgsfield directly; Muse never charges you.

1. Go to **Settings → Connections → Higgsfield**.
2. Press **Connect** and sign in in the window that opens.
3. When it says **Connected**, you're done.

To confirm it works without spending credits, run **Settings → System check**. It signs in and lists Higgsfield's tools.

If a generation later says your session expired, connect again from the same place.
