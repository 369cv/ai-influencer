Stats come from **Ayrshare**, for posts it sent. In **Content → Queue → Sent**, press **Refresh stats** to read the latest numbers. Each post shows views, likes, comments and saves when the network reports them.

Each influencer's profile has an **Insights** card with 30-day totals and her top posts.

Networks can take 24–48 hours to report numbers for new posts. Posts sent through Zapier, Make, n8n or a webhook show *can't report back*.
