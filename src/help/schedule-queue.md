Set an influencer's **posting schedule** on her profile or in **Settings → Social accounts**: a time zone, a weekly goal and times for each day.

In the composer, **Add to queue** puts the post in her next free slot. You can also pick a slot or set a custom time.

**Content → Queue** shows upcoming posts by day with empty slots to fill, plus **Drafts**, **Sent** and **Failed**. Failed posts show the reason and a **Retry** that only re-sends the routes that failed.
