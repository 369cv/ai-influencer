---
title: "A generation failed or stopped"
collection: "troubleshooting"
summary: "Session expired, nothing came back, or the page was closed."
cta: "Run System check"
to: "/settings?section=check"
---

**Higgsfield session expired:** reconnect in **Settings → Connections → Higgsfield**.

**Nothing came back:** the request may have been moderated or failed on Higgsfield. Check your Higgsfield dashboard; failed requests are usually not charged. Try rewording the scene.

**Stopped:** generations run in this tab. If you close or reload the page while one runs, it shows as *Stopped*. Open it and press **Make it** or **Redo** again.

Run **System check** to confirm Higgsfield answers.
