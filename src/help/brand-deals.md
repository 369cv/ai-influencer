On **Brand deals**, press **Deliverables** on a deal to set the influencer, fee and status, and add deliverables (Reel, Photo, Story, Post) with due dates.

- **Create post** opens the composer with `#ad @brand` and links the post to that deliverable.
- **Download approval pack** saves one web page with each item's media, caption and go-live time. Send it to the brand however you like.
- Record their answer: **Mark approved**, or **Changes requested** with their note. **Open in Studio** carries the note into the prompt.

Overdue and changes-requested deliverables appear in **Needs you** on Create.
